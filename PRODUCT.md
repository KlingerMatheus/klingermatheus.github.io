# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS, no JavaScript, no build step. Deployed by GitHub Pages from `main` (`.github/workflows/static.yml`). Published from `site/` only. Chosen by the owner on 2026-11-11 to replace a 1.3 MB design-tool bundle that only rendered with JavaScript.

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- Recruiters and tech leads at US and EU companies hiring a remote frontend engineer. They scan quickly for seniority, stack and experience.
- Product owners looking for a freelancer for a project. They look for shipped work and results.

Both leave by sending an email or opening LinkedIn/GitHub.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Klinger Matheus Vieira da Silva, Frontend Engineer (React and TypeScript). It exists to turn a visit into a contact. Success is a visitor who understands who he is, what he has shipped and how to reach him within one short read.

## Positioning

4+ years shipping production React/TypeScript applications, working asynchronously with US and EU teams, currently building AI workflow interfaces at Up Outsourcing. Open to remote contract, full-time, part-time and freelance work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One page per language: English at the root (default) and Portuguese at `/pt/`, linked by an EN/PT switch in the bar. No JavaScript; each language is its own static file, so a text change must be made in both.
- Sections in the current site: hero, technical stack, selected work, experience, about / how I work, contact.
- Must work without JavaScript and load fast on mobile.
- Light and dark themes following the visitor's system preference.
- Content source of truth: the owner's final data, kept in the Obsidian vault note `Portfolio/Reference - Portfolio Content (source of truth).md`.
- Résumé PDFs (English and Portuguese) ship from `site/`.
- Open: project screenshots have not been supplied.

## Product Principles

1. Reading comes first: every element either informs the visitor or gets removed.
2. Real work is the proof; claims without evidence stay out.
3. One clear way to get in touch, visible without hunting.
4. The page itself demonstrates the craft it describes: fast, accessible, no dependency it does not need.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in both themes, full keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
